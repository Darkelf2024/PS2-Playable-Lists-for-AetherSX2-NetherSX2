All games listed are classified as **Behaviorally Playable** based on real-world testing behavior, not strict numerical benchmarks.

- **PAL Builds:** Approximately **11–25 G**

Performance may vary depending on:
- Hardware
- Emulator version
- Game region
- Configuration settings

Minor timing or performance deviations may occur but are considered **within a stable operating range** when:
